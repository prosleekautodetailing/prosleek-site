FAQ

Before your detail.

Clear answers about mobile service, pricing, weather, and specialty work.

Is ProSleek mobile?

Yes. ProSleek provides mobile detailing at an agreed safe and legal service location.

What areas do you service?

Waukee and the Des Moines metro, including West Des Moines, Des Moines, Clive, Urbandale, Johnston, Ankeny, Norwalk, and Altoona.

Do you need access to water or electricity?

Access may be required depending on the service and mobile setup. Requirements are confirmed before the appointment.

Is a deposit required?

Standard appointments may require a $50 deposit. Large, specialty, marine, coating, correction, fleet, or high-value work may require a larger custom deposit agreed upon before booking.

What happens if it rains?

Rain, storms, extreme temperatures, or unsafe weather may require rescheduling. Weather rescheduling initiated by ProSleek transfers the deposit to the new appointment.

Are website prices final?

No. Advertised prices are starting prices. Any adjustment for size, condition, service requirements, or added labor is discussed and agreed upon before work begins.

How long does a detail take?

Timing depends on the selected service, vehicle size, condition, and additional work. ProSleek will provide expectations when reviewing the request.

How do maintenance plans work?

Begin with a qualifying initial detail, choose a biweekly or monthly interval, and keep the vehicle on the agreed schedule.

What happens if I miss a maintenance interval?

If the vehicle becomes heavily soiled or significantly exceeds the interval, added cleaning or regular-detail pricing may apply before maintenance pricing resumes.

Do you detail boats and powersports?

Yes. Marine, motorcycle, jet ski, and other powersports services are quoted based on size, layout, access, and condition.

How are correction and coating prices determined?

Pricing depends on paint or surface condition, size, correction and preparation required, desired finish, and coating selected. Photos or an in-person inspection may be required.
