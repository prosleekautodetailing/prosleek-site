Appointments

Deposits

Standard detailing appointments may require a $50 deposit to reserve the appointment. Large, specialty, marine, coating, correction, fleet, or high-value jobs may require a larger custom deposit agreed upon before booking.

Deposits are applied toward the final service balance. An appointment may not be considered fully reserved until any required deposit is received.

Cancellations / Rescheduling

Please cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours before the scheduled appointment. Late cancellations or rescheduling may result in forfeiture of the deposit. Weather-related rescheduling initiated by ProSleek transfers the deposit to the new appointment.

No-Shows

If the customer is unavailable or the vehicle cannot be accessed at the scheduled appointment time, the appointment may be treated as a no-show and the deposit may be forfeited. A new deposit may be required to reschedule.

Pricing

Final Pricing

Website prices are starting prices. Final pricing may vary based on vehicle size, condition, excessive pet hair, heavy stains, excessive trash, sand, mud, severe buildup, oversized or lifted vehicles, additional labor, and specialty correction or restoration requirements.

Any adjustment will be discussed and agreed upon before work begins.

Mobile Service

Location Requirements

The customer must provide a safe and legal location with enough space to perform the requested service. Access to water and electricity may be required depending on the service and mobile setup. Requirements will be confirmed before the appointment.

Weather

Rain, storms, extreme temperatures, or unsafe weather conditions may require rescheduling. Weather-related rescheduling initiated by ProSleek will not cause the customer to lose their deposit; it transfers to the new appointment.

Vehicle & Property

Personal Belongings

Please remove valuables, cash, medications, important documents, and personal belongings before service. ProSleek is not responsible for items left in the vehicle that are lost, damaged, or mistaken for trash.

Excessive Trash

Significant trash removal may result in an additional charge. ProSleek may decline to remove items that present a health or safety concern.

Biohazards / Unsafe Conditions

ProSleek reserves the right to refuse or stop service when unsafe materials or conditions are present, including bodily fluids, needles, human or animal waste, mold, pests, biological hazards, or other unsafe materials.

Pre-Existing Damage

ProSleek may document existing scratches, dents, chips, cracked trim, damaged upholstery, failing paint, loose components, or other visible defects. ProSleek is not responsible for pre-existing damage or deterioration that becomes more visible during normal cleaning or detailing.

Fragile / Aftermarket Components

Please disclose repainted panels, vinyl wraps, PPF, loose trim, aftermarket badges, custom interior pieces, repaired parts, aftermarket electronics, and fragile components before service.

Results Are Condition-Dependent

Complete removal of every stain, scratch, odor, swirl, water spot, oxidation mark, or paint defect cannot be guaranteed.

Specialty Services

Paint Correction

Results depend on paint type, paint thickness, defect severity, previous repairs, and overall condition. Some deeper scratches or defects may intentionally remain when safely removing them could compromise the paint.

Ceramic Coating

Pricing may require photos or an inspection before final confirmation. Coating performance and longevity depend on preparation, maintenance, environmental exposure, storage, and customer care. Coating life cannot be guaranteed regardless of maintenance.

Marine / Powersports

Boat, jet ski, motorcycle, and powersports pricing depends heavily on size, layout, oxidation, staining, surface condition, access, and required correction. Heavy oxidation, water spotting, gelcoat correction, sanding, polishing, or specialty restoration may require a revised estimate after inspection.

Aluminum / Metal Polishing

Pricing depends on size, oxidation, existing coatings, coating removal, sanding required, corrosion, pitting, surface condition, access, and desired finish. Final achievable results depend on the condition and type of metal.

Maintenance Plans

An initial qualifying detail is required before maintenance pricing begins. Interior Maintenance requires an Interior Detail or Full Detail first. Full Maintenance requires a Full Detail first.

Maintenance pricing applies while the vehicle remains on the agreed interval and in reasonably maintained condition. If it becomes heavily soiled or significantly exceeds the interval, additional cleaning charges or regular-detail pricing may apply before maintenance pricing resumes.

Payment & Photos

Payment

The remaining balance is due upon completion unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Photos / Marketing

ProSleek may photograph completed work for documentation or marketing. Personal information, home addresses, and identifying personal information will not be intentionally displayed. Customers may request that their vehicle not be used for promotional content.

Customer Concerns

If you have a concern about a completed service, please contact ProSleek promptly so there is an opportunity to review the concern and determine an appropriate response.
